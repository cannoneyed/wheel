## `focusScope`

## `Scrollbar`

Kind: function. Source: [packages/wheel/src/kit/layout/scrollbar.tsx:75](../../../packages/wheel/src/kit/layout/scrollbar.tsx#L75).

A permanent, framework-drawn scrollbar bound to one scroll container. It tracks the container's metrics through scroll events plus resize and mutation observers on the container AND its children, so the thumb grows and shrinks as panes are added, removed, or resized. The thumb drags through the same gesture machine as every other Wheel drag (Escape puts the scroll position back), the track pages on press, and the keyboard scrolls a focused bar. It renders as an in-flow gutter: place it after (axis `x`) or beside (axis `y`) the scroll container in a flex parent and it takes real layout space while something overflows, disappearing entirely otherwise — `Frame`'s `scrollable` prop does exactly that wiring. Theme via CSS custom properties: `--wheel-scrollbar-size`, `--wheel-scrollbar-track`, `--wheel-scrollbar-thumb`, and `--wheel-scrollbar-thumb-active`.
